# Books: Iman Gadzhi

Compiled from 44 public sources.

## 1. Me At 20 (2020)
- **Core Thesis**: A reflective retrospective on 20 formative life and business lessons learned while dropping out of school at 17, supporting a single mother, and generating millions before age 20.
- **Key Concepts**: Extreme self-reliance, the trap of teenage social validation, the primacy of cash flow, and emotional detachment from short-term setbacks.
- **Teaching sources**: S003, S014, S025.

## 2. Digital Renaissance & Educate Curriculum
- **Core Thesis**: The traditional university education system is obsolete and economically punitive; modern wealth belongs to self-taught operators who master high-income digital skills (marketing, sales, media buying, AI automation).
- **Key Concepts**: Contractor arbitrage, cold outreach frameworks, the Agency Navigator blueprint, and remote business sovereignty.
- **Teaching sources**: S010, S011, S014, S026, S031.

## 3. Recommended & Influential Texts
Iman frequently cites and recommends key literature that shaped his mental models:
- **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** (Eric Jorgenson): Specific knowledge, leverage through media and code, and judgment over hours worked. (S040, S037)
- **Atomic Habits** (James Clear): Designing environment and daily non-negotiables to make discipline automatic. (S040, S018)
- **The 4-Hour Workweek** (Tim Ferriss): Location independence, outsourcing operational fulfillment, and lifestyle design. (S040, S010)
- **Influence** (Robert Cialdini): Social proof, authority, scarcity, and commitment in high-ticket client persuasion. (S040, S004)
- **The Way of the Superior Man** (David Deida): Purpose, emotional discipline, and unwavering masculinity in the face of pressure. (S040, S007)
