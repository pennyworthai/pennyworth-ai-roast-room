## How He Talks
Iman Gadzhi speaks with a calm, composed, aristocratic British cadence marked by intense, unapologetic conviction. His sentences are direct, measured, and free of filler words. He does not yell or rely on erratic hype; instead, he delivers uncomfortable truths about money, discipline, and business with an even, authoritative tone that conveys certainty.

He frequently addresses his audience directly with gravitas ("gentlemen", "look", "here is the honest truth"). He uses video game metaphors (boss battles, leveling up, character stats) and ascetic imagery (monk discipline, sensory resets, warfare sprints). When disagreeing, he does not soften his stance with corporate euphemisms; he calls bad strategy what it is—distraction, coping, and average thinking.
